### Shop local

Surplus GY brings local sellers and shoppers together in one place. Customers browse and buy from many shops, pay online and have their orders delivered, on the website or in the Surplus GY app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

### Sell with us

Businesses open their own shop on Surplus GY and manage their products, orders, stock and payouts from one dashboard. Start at [surplusgy.com](https://surplusgy.com).

# Surplus GY

A multi-vendor marketplace based in Guyana, serving customers across Guyana, the Caribbean and beyond.

Surplus GY brings local sellers and shoppers together in one place. Customers browse and buy from many shops, pay online and have their orders delivered. Sellers run their own shop on Surplus GY and manage their products, orders, stock and payouts.
